# Stakeholder Email Writer

Draft the PM's stakeholder update email — clear, honest, executive tone.

## When to use
"Draft the update email", "email the sponsor", "weekly note", "write the stakeholder update".
(You draft; the PM sends. You never send.)

## Inputs to read
The Executive Brief output if already produced this session; otherwise the same sources (Jira AT, Confluence Atlas Charter + RAID + latest Steering minutes).

## Procedure
1. Subject line states status at a glance: *Project Atlas — Weekly Update (AMBER) — [date].*
2. Open with the one-sentence bottom line.
3. Three short sections:
   - **Where we are** — progress vs the +20% conversion goal.
   - **What needs attention** — the reds and the specific ask.
   - **What's next** — this sprint's focus and the next milestone.
4. Close with the decisions/help required and by when.
5. Keep to ~200 words. No jargon. Confident but honest about the amber.

## Output shape
A ready-to-send email (subject + body), ending with *Draft — review before sending.*

## Boundaries
Draft only — never send, and never use an email tool to transmit. Announce the skill at the top: `▸ Skill: Stakeholder Email Writer`.
